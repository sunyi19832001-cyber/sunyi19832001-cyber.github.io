个人简介Biography

孙益，中医骨伤科学博士，主任中医师。浙江中医药大学七年制本硕连读毕业，后于同校获中医骨伤科学博士学位；2009 年起在余姚市中医医院工作至今，现任综合康复科主任，长期从事中西医结合骨伤与康复疾病的临床、科研与教学工作。

浙江省中青年临床名中医，入选第六批全国中医临床优秀人才研修项目培养对象；任余姚市医学重点学科（中医康复学）学科带头人，带领 11 人康复团队，并设有浙江省中青年临床名中医传承工作室。师从名老中医李义方，长期跟师学习，主编《李义芳骨伤科疾病经验集》。

Yi Sun, MD, PhD, is a chief physician of Traditional Chinese Medicine specializing in TCM orthopedics and traumatology. He completed the seven-year integrated Bachelor–Master program at Zhejiang Chinese Medical University and later earned his PhD in TCM Orthopedics & Traumatology from the same university. He has practiced at Yuyao Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine since 2009 and currently serves as Director of the Department of Comprehensive Rehabilitation.

He is a Zhejiang Provincial Young and Middle-Aged Famous TCM Physician and was selected for the Sixth National TCM Clinical Excellence Talent Training Program. As discipline leader of the Yuyao Municipal Key Medical Discipline (TCM Rehabilitation), he leads an 11-member rehabilitation team and hosts the Sun Yi TCM Clinical Inheritance Studio. He apprenticed under the renowned senior TCM physician Li Yifang and served as editor-in-chief of a volume documenting Li's clinical experience in orthopedic diseases.

研究方向Research Interests

研究方向Research Interests

痛风与高尿酸血症防治Gout & Hyperuricemia

中西医结合防治痛风性关节炎：体质辨证与循经辨证相结合，关注降尿酸治疗启动期发作的预防。Integrative prevention and treatment of gouty arthritis, combining constitution-based and meridian-based pattern differentiation, with attention to flare prevention when urate-lowering therapy is initiated.

红外光谱与代谢标志物Infrared Spectroscopy & Metabolic Biomarkers

以 ATR-FTIR 血清光谱指纹探索高尿酸血症人群痛风首次发作的早期标志物与风险分层。Serum ATR-FTIR spectral fingerprinting to identify early biomarkers and risk stratification for first gout flares in people with hyperuricemia.

肠道菌群与方药机制Gut Microbiota & Formula Mechanisms

基于高通量测序研究中药复方（如四妙散加减方）治疗痛风性关节炎的肠道菌群作用机制。High-throughput sequencing studies of how herbal formulas (e.g., modified Simiao San) act on the gut microbiota in gouty arthritis.

临床预测模型Clinical Prediction Models

面向筋骨疾病与代谢性疾病的临床预测模型构建与验证，及系统评价 / Meta 分析。Development and validation of clinical prediction models for musculoskeletal and metabolic diseases, alongside systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

写作与科普Writing & Outreach

写作与科普Medical Writing & Public Outreach

创立「孙益医生」医学科普专栏（微信公众号「孙益名医工作室」、今日头条），持续撰写骨伤康复与慢性病科普文章，原创 750 余篇；参与编著《骨科常见疾病保健手册》等科普读物，并任宁波市健康教育巡讲师，长期开展健康科普讲座。

同时从事英文医学写作，面向国际读者介绍中医药在骨伤与康复领域的应用，包括痛风的夜间发作机制、膝骨关节炎的中西医结合诊治等临床主题。

He writes the “Dr. Sun Yi” medical popular-science column (WeChat Official Account “孙益名医工作室” and Toutiao), with more than 750 original articles on musculoskeletal rehabilitation and chronic disease care, and contributed to popular health handbooks on common orthopedic conditions. He also serves as a Ningbo municipal health-education lecturer.

In English, he writes for international readers on Traditional Chinese Medicine in orthopedics and rehabilitation — including the mechanisms behind nocturnal gout flares and integrative approaches to knee osteoarthritis.
